## Game Overview

**Title:** NeoScav  
**Genre:** 3D Puzzle / Logic Game  
**Platform:** PC  
**Audience:** Ages 15–30, puzzle-solving enthusiasts

**Summary:**  
In NeoScav, you solve spatial puzzles by interacting with platforms, blocks, and triggers. Your main goal is to reach the green teleporter that transports you to the next level.

**Key Features:**
- Intuitive yet challenging puzzle mechanics  
- Two game versions (with and without collectibles)

## Gameplay & Mechanics

1. **Core Gameplay Loop**  
   - Enter the level and analyze puzzle elements.  
   - Manipulate objects (e.g., blocks, cannons, battery blocks) and activate mechanisms (pressure plates, locks, moving platforms).  
   - Traverse the environment to reach the green teleporter.  
   - Advance to the next level as new mechanics come into play.

2. **Character Abilities**  
   - **Movement:** Move from a top-down perspective.  
   - **Carrying:** Pick up certain blocks and carry them on your head.

3. **Interactive Elements**  
   - **Standard Block:** Can be pushed, pulled, or carried.  
   - **Battery Block:** Activates specified locks within range; can also be carried.  
   - **Cannon:** Shoots projectiles to temporarily activate locks; can be carried.  
   - **Pressure Plate:** Triggered by weight (player or block).  
   - **Descending Block:** Moves vertically when activated. (Negative movement may move it upward.)  
   - **Moving Platform:** Travels along set coordinates once triggered.  
   - **Bouncing Plate:** Propels objects/players vertically.  
   - **Lock:** Activation triggers one or more linked mechanisms.  
   - **Bullet Barrage Mechanism:** Requires dodging bullets.  
   - **Green Teleporter:** Goal portal to the next level.

## Game Versions

1. **Standard Version:**  
   Focus on puzzle-solving with no collectibles.

2. **Collectible Version:**  
   Adds hidden items to each level for an extra layer of challenge.

## Controls

- **Movement Keys:** Move the character in the four directions.  
- **Interact / Pick Up / Put Down:** Use the **Space** key or left-click to pick up/place down blocks or interact with puzzle elements.
